## Statement

A [gate](../../docs/TERMS.md#gate) fails the change if invoice-equivalent money math, status transitions, or named adjectives appear outside the owning [noun](../../docs/TERMS.md#noun) (copy-paste of the adjective).

## Binary criteria

[Met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met) iff `tools/fitness-adjective-locality.py` exits 0 (`RESULT:MET`) on the scanned tree.
Not [met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met) iff it prints `VIOLATION` and exits 1.

## V1 scope

- Tokens in `domain/<noun>/adjectives.txt` must not appear as string literals in `goals/` / `adapters/`.
- Declared fields in `fields.txt` must not be used with arithmetic or comparison operators outside the [noun](../../docs/TERMS.md#noun) (`invoice.balance - amount`, `invoice.status ==`).

Field *assignment* (`invoice.status =`) remains R5 / C4. Persistence (`.save(`) remains R6 v1.

Known-fail: `examples/invoice-locality-violation/`.
Known-pass: `examples/invoice-correct/` (no tokens, no field math in the goal).

Matrix binder is this tool.
