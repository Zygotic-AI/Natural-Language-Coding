# A-R32

- Requirement: `R32`
- Outcome: **met** | **not met** only
- Gate: `tools/fitness-taint-lifetime.py`

## Statement

A sensitive adjective listed in `taint.txt` may cross one boundary: the consuming verb. It is not returned, stored on another object, or passed to another boundary.

## V2 bind

Tokens: `domain/<noun>/taint.txt`. Inside one consuming function, names
assigned from a token or from a `get_`/`read_`/`export_` call are tainted.
Fails return / field-store / pass of those names.

Same-file and other files under the scan root. Out of reach: third-party packages.



Designed fail: `examples/card-taint-violation/` (return token by name),
`examples/card-taint-alias/` (`n = get_number(); return n`).
Designed pass: `examples/invoice-correct/` (no taint.txt → skip).
