## Statement

Goals may call goals through public contracts. There is no separate [workflow](../../docs/TERMS.md#workflow) citizen. A durable runtime hosts a [goal](../../docs/TERMS.md#goal); it does not replace the [goal](../../docs/TERMS.md#goal).

## V1 bind

A directory under `workflows/` with both an entrypoint (`implementation.py` /
`run.py` / `main.py`) *and* `input.schema.json` or `output.schema.json` is a
cloned [goal](../../docs/TERMS.md#goal).

A thin host file with no [goal](../../docs/TERMS.md#goal) schemas is allowed. `workflows/` may exist.

Does not [prove](../../docs/TERMS.md#prove) a Temporal worker is a host.

Designed fail: `examples/workflow-as-goal/`.
Designed pass: `examples/invoice-correct/` (no workflows/).
