- [Requirement](../../docs/TERMS.md#requirement): `CS4`
- Outcome: **[met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met)** | **not [met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met)** only

## Statement

Success criteria are binary (ops vs defects).

## Binary criteria

[Met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met) iff the changed [agent noun](../../docs/TERMS.md#agent-noun) has a success criteria table with ops/defect columns and binary outcomes. Not [met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met) if success criteria are missing, vague, or non-binary.

## Evidence

On [met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met) or not [met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met), cite `agents/<name>/AGENT.md` success criteria section.
