# A-R18

- Requirement: `R18`
- Outcome: **met** | **not met** only
- Gate: `tools/fitness-r18-noun-retries.py`

## Statement

Compensation and retries live in the calling goal (and its runtime if durable), not inside the noun.

## V1 bind

Noun source must not contain `temporalio`, `retry_policy`, `backoff`, `compensate(`,
`@workflow`, or `for attempt in`.

Does not prove the goal owns the retry. That is C15.

Designed fail: `examples/noun-retries/`.
Designed pass: `examples/invoice-correct/`.
