# A-R18

- [Requirement](../../docs/TERMS.md#requirement): `R18`
- Outcome: **[met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met)** | **not [met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met)** only
- [Gate](../../docs/TERMS.md#gate): `tools/fitness-r18-noun-retries.py`

## Statement

Compensation and retries live in the calling [goal](../../docs/TERMS.md#goal) (and its runtime if durable), not inside the [noun](../../docs/TERMS.md#noun).

## V1 bind

[Noun](../../docs/TERMS.md#noun) source must not contain `temporalio`, `retry_policy`, `backoff`, `compensate(`,
`@workflow`, or `for attempt in`.

Does not [prove](../../docs/TERMS.md#prove) the [goal](../../docs/TERMS.md#goal) owns the retry. That is C15.

Designed fail: `examples/noun-retries/`.
Designed pass: `examples/invoice-correct/`.
