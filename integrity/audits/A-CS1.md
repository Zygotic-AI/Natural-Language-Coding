# A-CS1

- [Requirement](../../docs/TERMS.md#requirement): `CS1`
- Outcome: **[met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met)** | **not [met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met)** only

## Statement

[Agent noun](../../docs/TERMS.md#agent-noun) has identity and adjectives.

## Binary criteria

[Met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met) iff the changed or new [agent noun](../../docs/TERMS.md#agent-noun) package contains an `AGENT.md` with identity (name, purpose) and adjectives list. Not [met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met) if identity or adjectives are missing from any changed [agent noun](../../docs/TERMS.md#agent-noun).

## Evidence

On [met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met) or not [met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met), cite `agents/<name>/AGENT.md` identity and adjectives sections.
