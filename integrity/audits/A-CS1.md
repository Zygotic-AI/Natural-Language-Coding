# A-CS1

- Requirement: `CS1`
- Outcome: **met** | **not met** only

## Statement

Agent noun has identity and adjectives.

## Binary criteria

Met iff the changed or new agent noun package contains an `AGENT.md` with identity (name, purpose) and adjectives list. Not met if identity or adjectives are missing from any changed agent noun.

## Evidence

On met or not met, cite `agents/<name>/AGENT.md` identity and adjectives sections.
