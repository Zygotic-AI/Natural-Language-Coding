# A-C13

- Requirement: `C13`
- Outcome: **met** | **not met** only
- Gate: `tools/fitness-c13.py`

## Statement

No new goal exists whose only job is a single noun-verb with no I/O or policy — or an ADR explains why the wrapper exists.

## V1 bind

A goal directory with `.py` that makes exactly one `.verb(` call and has
neither `input.schema.json` nor `output.schema.json` fails.

Schemas present = I/O; allowed. That is why `invoice-correct` passes.
Does not parse an ADR exception.

Designed fail: `examples/wrapper-goal/`.
Designed pass: `examples/invoice-correct/`.
