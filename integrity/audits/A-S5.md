# A-S5

- [Requirement](../../docs/TERMS.md#requirement): `S5`
- Outcome: **[met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met)** | **not [met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met)** only

## Statement

[Produce ≠ Audit ≠ Ship](../../docs/TERMS.md#produce-audit-ship). An agent may not [audit](../../docs/TERMS.md#audit) its own output as the final [gate](../../docs/TERMS.md#gate).

## Binary criteria

[Met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met) iff:
1. [Agent noun](../../docs/TERMS.md#agent-noun) packages do not grant the same agent both produce and [audit](../../docs/TERMS.md#audit) authority for the same artifact
2. No [agent noun](../../docs/TERMS.md#agent-noun) is documented as both producer and final auditor of its output
3. Verb lists show clear separation of produce, [audit](../../docs/TERMS.md#audit), and [ship](../../docs/TERMS.md#ship) functions

Not [met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met) if any [agent noun](../../docs/TERMS.md#agent-noun) can both produce and self-audit as final [gate](../../docs/TERMS.md#gate), or if an [audit](../../docs/TERMS.md#audit) role has [ship](../../docs/TERMS.md#ship) authority.

## Evidence

On [met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met) or not [met](../../docs/TERMS.md#met), cite `agents/<name>/AGENT.md` shipping authority section and verb list exclusions. Trace produce → [audit](../../docs/TERMS.md#audit) → [ship](../../docs/TERMS.md#ship) path.
